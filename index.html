Mochi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Mochi is a watch face for Garmin watches, published by Small Hours Apps (smallhourslabs@gmail.com).

What Mochi reads, on the watch only

  • Body Battery, stress and Sleep Score, through Garmin's complications (the "Complications" permission).
  • Your sleep and wake times from your Garmin user profile (the "User profile" permission).
  • Steps, step goal, heart rate, calories, active minutes, battery level and notification count, through standard watch APIs.

These values are used only to draw the face and choose your companion's mood.

What Mochi stores

Your face settings, which Garmin syncs through the Connect IQ app. On the watch itself: a small sticker-book record (today's date, whether today's step goal was reached, sticker total, days together).

What Mochi collects

Nothing. It makes no network requests, has no analytics or ads, and sends no data to the publisher or any third party.

Deleting your data

Removing Mochi from your watch removes its settings and sticker book.
